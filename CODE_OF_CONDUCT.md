# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and respectful.

- Be kind, constructive and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Do not harass, insult, discriminate against or threaten contributors.
- Follow maintainer instructions and keep discussions focused on the project.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions that do not meet these expectations.
